Deleting your account

This page explains how to delete your account and cloud save in Star Jars Sort Deluxe (Yıldız Kavanozu in Turkish), a game by Symbol Interactive. Developer: Cem Tezgelen (Symbol Interactive).

In the game

  1. Open Settings in the game.
  2. Tap Cloud save.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and cloud save are deleted at once. The progress on this phone stays and the game carries on with it; to remove that too, uninstall the game.

By email

If you can't open the game, or would rather we did it, email symbolinteractive@gmail.com with the subject line Account deletion and include the player ID shown in the game's Settings. We find your account by that ID.

If you can't see your ID because you deleted the game: if you linked your account to Apple or Google, install the game again and sign in with the same account; your ID appears in Settings again, and you can delete the account there yourself. If you never linked it, the anonymous account belonged only to the game on that phone and we cannot tell which account is yours; such an account is deleted on its own after 24 months without use.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save: your save document in Cloud Firestore, with your progress, settings, progress summary, device model and save times.
  • Your Firebase account: your anonymous account ID and, if you linked one, its link to your Apple or Google account.

A deleted cloud save cannot be brought back. Permanent purchases such as Remove Ads belong to your store account; you can restore them with the same App Store or Google Play account.

What remains

  • Analytics and crash data: records in Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are tied not to your game account but to an ID unique to the game's copy on your phone, and they are deleted on their own: Analytics events after 14 months, crash logs after 90 days. Aggregate, anonymous figures made from them that identify no one (daily player counts, for example) remain.
  • Ad partners' data: Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads) and Google AdMob keep and delete what they processed for ads under their own policies: Unity, Google. This information is not tied to your game account; you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Store purchase records: Apple and Google keep records of your purchases under their own policies.
  • Emails: if you wrote to us to delete your account, the emails we exchanged are deleted within a year of the matter being closed.

How long it takes

Deletion is done within 30 days. In the game, your account and cloud save are deleted at once; by email, we delete them within 30 days and let you know when it's done. Google separately clears copies of deleted data from its backup systems on its own schedule.

How your information is processed is in the privacy policy. Questions: symbolinteractive@gmail.com.
