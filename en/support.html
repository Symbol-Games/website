Support

Something wrong, or an idea to share? Write to symbolinteractive@gmail.com. Telling us the game, your phone model and what happened helps us help you sooner.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. You don't need an account. If you delete the game, your progress goes with it.

Something I bought is missing

Permanent purchases, such as removing ads, come back when you install the game again with the same App Store or Google Play account. If one doesn't, write to us with the purchase date and the receipt the store sent you.

Refunds

The stores take the payments and handle refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, and Google Play's refund page for Google Play.

Deleting my data

Deleting the game removes your progress. To have data held by the ad and analytics services deleted, write to us; the details are in the privacy policy.
