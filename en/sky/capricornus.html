Capricornus

Capricornus: Neptune was found by its brightest star in 1846.

Best in September, around 9 p.m.No. 40 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Deneb Algedi (magnitude 2.81)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

The smallest of the zodiac: its brighter stars draw a triangle (Algedi, Deneb Algedi and Omega). Between Sagittarius and Aquarius, low in the south on September evenings.

Legend

A sea-goat, half goat and half fish; the Babylonians knew it as “the Goat-Fish”, the sign of their god Ea. In the Greek tale it is Pan, who gave himself a fish’s tail and dived into a river when the monster Typhon attacked. In others it is Amalthea, the goat that nursed baby Zeus; her broken horn became the horn of plenty.

Sign

22 December to 19 January. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Capricornus from about 19 January to 16 February.
