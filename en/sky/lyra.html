Lyra

Lyra: Vega was the first star photographed after the Sun.

Best in August, around 9 p.m.No. 52 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Vega (magnitude 0.03)6 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Vega, one of the night’s brightest stars, is a corner of the Summer Triangle. Beside it four faint stars make a small parallelogram, the lyre’s frame. On August evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth Lyra is the lyre of Orpheus, whose music charmed even stones. Sailing with the Argonauts, he played over the Sirens’ song and saved the ship.
