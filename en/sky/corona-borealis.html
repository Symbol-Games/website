Corona Borealis

Corona Borealis: the Blaze Star flared up in 1866 and 1946.

Best in June, around 9 p.m.No. 73 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Alphecca (magnitude 2.24)6 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

A small semicircle of stars between Boötes and Hercules, bright Alphecca at its middle. On June evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth it is the crown the god Dionysus gave the Cretan princess Ariadne, and she set it in the heavens. Other cultures saw a circle of elders, an eagle’s nest, a bear’s den or a smokehole.

Its stars
