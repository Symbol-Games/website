Gemini

Gemini: Castor looks like one light but is six stars.

Best in February, around 9 p.m.No. 30 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Pollux (magnitude 1.14)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Two bright stars side by side, Castor and Pollux, are the twins’ heads; their bodies run down from them in two parallel lines. Between Taurus and Cancer, on February evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth Castor and Pollux are twins: Pollux the immortal son of Zeus, Castor the mortal one, and Pollux shared his immortality with his brother. The Babylonians called the two stars the Great Twins.

Sign

22 May to 21 June. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Gemini from about 21 June to 21 July.

Its stars

Magnitude says how bright a star looks from here: the smaller the number, the brighter the star.
