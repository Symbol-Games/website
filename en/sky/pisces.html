Pisces

Pisces: the Sun stands before it at the March equinox today.

Best in November, around 9 p.m.No. 14 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Alpherg (magnitude 3.61)9 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Two distant fishes, each on a cord that meets at one end: the western fish’s head is a small ring, and the knot is Alrescha. Between Aquarius and Aries, on November evenings.

Legend

In the Greek tale Aphrodite and her son Eros turned into fishes to escape the monster Typhon, or two fishes rescued them. The Babylonians saw here “the great swallow” and the “fish cord” that ties the two fishes.

Sign

19 February to 20 March. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Pisces from about 12 March to 19 April.
