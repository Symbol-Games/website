Aquila

Aquila: Altair is the Summer Triangle’s nearest corner.

Best in August, around 9 p.m.No. 22 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Altair (magnitude 0.76)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Bright Altair, a corner of the Summer Triangle, with Tarazed and Alshain either side of it in a short line. On August evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth the eagle carries Zeus’s thunderbolts. It is also the eagle that carried Ganymede, a son of a king of Troy, up to Olympus to be the gods’ cup-bearer.
