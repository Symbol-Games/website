Sagittarius

Sagittarius: its stars draw a teapot.

Best in August, around 9 p.m.No. 15 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Kaus Australis (magnitude 1.85)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Its bright stars draw a teapot; just off its spout lies the centre of the Milky Way. Low in the south on summer evenings.

Legend

Sagittarius is drawn as a centaur or a satyr with his bow drawn. Eratosthenes held him to be Crotus, said to have invented archery, set in the sky at the Muses’ wish.

Sign

22 November to 21 December. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Sagittarius from about 18 December to 18 January; it is there at the December solstice.
