Leo

First stop on the zodiac: a sickle-shaped mane.

Best in April, around 9 p.m.No. 12 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Regulus (magnitude 1.35)9 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

The Sickle, a backwards question mark for its mane, with bright Regulus at its foot.

Legend

The first of Heracles’ twelve labours is the Nemean lion. No weapon can pierce its hide, so he overcomes it with his bare hands, and Zeus sets the lion in the sky.

Sign

23 July to 22 August. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Leo from about 10 August to 16 September.

Its stars

Magnitude says how bright a star looks from here: the smaller the number, the brighter the star.
