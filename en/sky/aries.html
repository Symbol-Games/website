Aries

Aries: the spring equinox point bears its name, now in Pisces.

Best in December, around 9 p.m.No. 39 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Hamal (magnitude 2.00)7 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

The ram’s head: three stars in a short bent line, Hamal, Sheratan and Mesarthim. Between Pisces and Taurus, on December evenings.

Legend

In the Greek tale it is a flying ram with a golden fleece: on Hermes’ orders it rescued Phrixus and Helle and carried Phrixus to Colchis. Helle fell in flight, and the Dardanelles took her name, the Hellespont. The Babylonians saw a ram here, and a hired farm labourer.

Sign

21 March to 20 April. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Aries from about 19 April to 14 May.
