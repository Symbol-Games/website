Cygnus

Cygnus: Deneb is a corner of the Summer Triangle.

Best in September, around 9 p.m.No. 16 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Deneb (magnitude 1.25)6 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

A great cross along the Milky Way, the Northern Cross: bright Deneb at its top, Albireo at its foot. On September evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth Zeus takes the form of a swan to reach Leda. In another tale Cycnus dives into the river again and again to find his fallen friend Phaethon, and Zeus, in pity, turns him into a swan.

Its stars

Magnitude says how bright a star looks from here: the smaller the number, the brighter the star.
