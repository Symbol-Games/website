Scorpius

Scorpius: red supergiant Antares is its heart.

Best in July, around 9 p.m.No. 33 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Antares (magnitude 1.06)10 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

A hooked tail with red Antares at its heart; Shaula and Lesath mark the tail’s tip. Low in the south on summer evenings.

Legend

When Orion boasts he will kill every animal, Artemis and Leto send a giant scorpion to kill him. Zeus sets both in the sky, a reminder to mortals to curb their pride.

Sign

23 October to 21 November. In the real sky the Sun crosses Scorpius for only about a week, 23 to 29 November; then until 17 December it is in front of Ophiuchus, the Serpent Bearer.
