Great Bear

Ursa Major

The Big Dipper: seven stars that point north.

Best in April, around 9 p.m.No. 3 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Alioth (magnitude 1.77)7 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Draw a line from Merak to Dubhe at the end of the Dipper’s bowl and go on five times as far: you reach the Pole Star. Seen all year from mid-northern latitudes.

Legend

Zeus falls for Callisto, a nymph of Artemis; when she bears him a son, Arcas, Hera turns her into a bear. Zeus at last lifts mother and son into the sky: Callisto as the Great Bear, Arcas as Boötes.

Its stars

Magnitude says how bright a star looks from here: the smaller the number, the brighter the star.
