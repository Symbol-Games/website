Taurus

Taurus: the Crab Nebula’s supernova was seen by day in 1054.

Best in January, around 9 p.m.No. 17 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Aldebaran (magnitude 0.85)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

The bull’s face is a V of stars, the Hyades, with bright orange Aldebaran for its eye. The Pleiades sit on its back, on January evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth it is Zeus, who took the form of a magnificent white bull to carry off Europa, a Phoenician princess. Many cultures call the Pleiades on its back the “seven sisters”.

Sign

21 April to 21 May. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Taurus from about 14 May to 21 June.
