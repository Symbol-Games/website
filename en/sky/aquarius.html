Aquarius

Aquarius: the Helix Nebula, the nearest planetary nebula to us.

Best in October, around 9 p.m.No. 10 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Sadalsuud (magnitude 2.87)9 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Find the Y-shaped Water Jar: Sadachbia, Zeta Aquarii and Fenmu are among its stars. Between Capricornus and Pisces, in the south on October evenings.

Legend

The Babylonians knew it as “the Great One”: the god Ea himself, holding an overflowing jar. In the Greek tale it is Ganymede, the handsome youth who poured the gods’ drink. Four of its stars draw a Y-shaped “Water Jar”.

Sign

20 January to 19 February. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Aquarius from about 16 February to 12 March.
