Orion

His three-star belt is winter’s easiest find.

Best in January, around 9 p.m.No. 26 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Rigel (magnitude 0.13)7 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

The three stars of its Belt in a row: Alnitak, Alnilam, Mintaka. The Belt points southeast to Sirius and northwest to Aldebaran. The constellation of winter evenings.

Legend

When the giant hunter Orion boasts he will kill every animal on Earth, a scorpion is sent to sting him. So Orion and Scorpius never share the sky: as one rises, the other sets.
