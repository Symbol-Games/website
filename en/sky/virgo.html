Virgo

Virgo: home of Spica, a hot blue star.

Best in May, around 9 p.m.No. 2 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Spica (magnitude 0.97)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Follow the arc of the Dipper’s handle to Arcturus, and speed on to Spica.

Legend

Most often Virgo is Astraea, goddess of justice, the last to leave the Earth when people grew wicked. The ear of wheat in her hand, Spica, stands for the harvest; some tales tie her to Demeter and her daughter Persephone.

Sign

23 August to 22 September. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Virgo from about 16 September to 30 October: at 44 days, the longest of the zodiac constellations.
