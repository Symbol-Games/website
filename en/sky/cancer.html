Cancer

Cancer: the Copernicus system has at least seven planets.

Best in March, around 9 p.m.No. 31 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Tarf (magnitude 3.53)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

A faint constellation between Gemini and Leo, invisible under city lights. At its middle the Beehive Cluster glows like a mist in a dark sky. On March evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth Hera sends a giant crab against Heracles as he fights the Lernaean Hydra. The crab bites his foot and is crushed, and Hera sets it among the stars.

Sign

22 June to 22 July. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Cancer from about 21 July to 10 August.

Its stars

Magnitude says how bright a star looks from here: the smaller the number, the brighter the star.
