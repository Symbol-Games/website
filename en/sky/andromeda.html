Andromeda

Andromeda: its galaxy’s light set off 2.5 million years ago.

Best in November, around 9 p.m.No. 19 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Alpheratz (magnitude 2.06)8 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Alpheratz, the upper left corner of the Great Square of Pegasus, is Andromeda’s head; from it Mirach and Almach run east like a chain. November evenings.

Legend

In Greek myth Andromeda is the daughter of Queen Cassiopeia. After her mother’s boast angered Poseidon, she was chained to a rock for his sea monster; the hero Perseus saved her and married her.
