Libra

Libra: its stars were once the Scorpion’s claws.

Best in June, around 9 p.m.No. 29 of the 88 constellations by areaBrightest star Zubeneschamali (magnitude 2.61)5 stars in this guide

The drawings on these maps are the game’s, and don’t show exact positions in the sky.

Find it in the sky

Between Virgo and Scorpius, four stars drawing a quadrangle: the beam and pans of the scales.

Legend

In ancient Greece Libra’s stars were the Scorpion’s claws. The Romans saw them as the scales of Astraea, goddess of justice, held by her neighbour Virgo.

Sign

23 September to 22 October. In the real sky the Sun passes in front of Libra from about 31 October to 22 November.
