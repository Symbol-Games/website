About

Symbol Interactive is an independent studio making games that open in a few minutes on your phone, are made with care, and leave you feeling good.

Our vision

Most mobile games shout for your attention. We try the opposite: calm, clear games that feel satisfying to touch, a fair economy, and respect for players' information. A few games, each polished for a long time, rather than a big catalogue.

How we work

  • Calm and satisfying

    We design relaxing puzzles that fit into short sessions. Every move has a visible result, and every chapter a good ending.

  • Our own art and sound

    We make our games' visuals, animation and sound effects ourselves. We don't use ready-made art packs.

  • Fair monetisation

    Our games are free. Watching a rewarded ad is your choice, there are no banner ads, and every shop pack says plainly what it gives.

  • Respect for privacy

    We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play. We say plainly what we process and why in our privacy policy.

Who

Cem Tezgelen founded Symbol Interactive in Aydın, Türkiye, in 2026. Symbol Interactive is not a company name but the name under which Cem Tezgelen publishes his games; he is also the controller of the personal data in our games (privacy policy).
