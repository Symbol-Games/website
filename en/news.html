What’s new

What has been added to Star Jars as it gets ready for release? Newest first.

  1. Version0.80

    • Tonight’s sky now sits up top; the arrow hides it
    • Unfold your phone mid-level and the jars rearrange

  2. Version0.79

    • Your observatory now stands on the home screen

  3. Version0.77

    • Home now fits tablets and foldable phones

  4. Version0.72

    • Bosses are new, with new rules

  5. Version0.71

    • Catch special moments, earn new avatars
    • Badges and jar themes have arrived
    • The Observatory is open: build your hill, upgrade your telescope

  6. Version0.70

    • Countdown: see where and when it happens
    • Real sky events: a reminder, the facts and a special chapter
    • Sky window: the stars you light, in the real sky

  7. Version0.69

    • Night watch: a tower that grows floor by floor after level 150, no lives spent
    • Star Road: a 28-day season with 30 steps of free prizes
    • Three new special chapters: white dwarf, red dwarf, black hole

  8. Version0.68

    • Add friends and send each other lives
    • World and friends rankings for the daily puzzle

  9. Version0.67

    • The daily puzzle is now very hard: few solutions, unlimited tries
    • Share your tries as moon phases, with no spoilers

  10. Version0.65

    • Rewards now arrive with a real celebration
    • You can enlarge the text in Settings

  11. Version0.64

    • Bigger login calendar: day 30 and day 365 prizes
    • A new opening: the jar fills, the game begins

  12. Version0.63

    • Cloud save: your progress follows you to a new phone

  13. Version0.61

    • New constellation: Andromeda
    • New special chapters: Ice giant and Helix Nebula

  14. Version0.57

    • New constellation: Lyra, with the clock star
    • New special chapters: Globular cluster and Galactic centre

  15. Version0.55

    • New constellation: Aquila
    • New special chapters: Galaxy and Exoplanet

  16. Version0.54

    • New constellation: Cancer, with one-way jars
    • New special chapters: Supernova and Meteor shower

  17. Version0.53

    • New constellation: Cygnus

  18. Version0.52

    • Now in Arabic

  19. Version0.51

    • Colour-blind dots on tags and guides too
    • Now in Japanese, Korean and Chinese

  20. Version0.50

    • A colour-blind mode in the settings
    • The game now speaks 9 languages
    • A Legendary level in every constellation
