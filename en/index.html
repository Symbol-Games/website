What’s in the box

No timers, no rush: play at your own pace.

A

Light up real constellations

150+ levels across 20+ real constellations, from the Great Bear to the Eagle, every star by its real name.

B

A card for every star

Its distance, colour, brightness and what its name means. Every star has a true story.

C

A jar for every constellation

Each chapter ends with a jar drawn for its constellation, like the Eagle’s winged jar.

D

New ideas along the way

Icy, labelled and locked jars, joker stars, clock stars, move limits, bound stars, upside-down and one-way jars.

E

Space chapters with their own rules

A nebula, Saturn’s ring, the Betelgeuse supernova, the Leonid meteors, the Sombrero Galaxy and Gliese 581.

F

A new puzzle every day

A daily puzzle, daily quests, streak rewards and a piggy bank.

G

Fill your shelf of stars

Every sky object you win stays with you.

H

Everyone plays

Colour-blind mode: every colour has its own shape and mark. Three text sizes. Plays offline; cloud save is optional.

How we make games

  1. Satisfying to touch

    Every move has a visible result, and every chapter a good ending.

  2. Our own art and sound

    We make our games’ visuals, animation and sound effects ourselves. We don’t use ready-made art packs.

  3. Fair monetisation

    Our games are free. Watching a rewarded ad is your choice, there are no banner ads, and every shop pack says plainly what it gives.

  4. Respect for privacy

    We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play. We say plainly what we process and why in our privacy policy.
