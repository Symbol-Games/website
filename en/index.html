How we make games

We are a small studio, so we polish each game for a long time before we release it.

  • Calm and satisfying

    We design relaxing puzzles that fit into short sessions. Every move has a visible result, and every chapter a good ending.

  • Our own art and sound

    We make our games' visuals, animation and sound effects ourselves. We don't use ready-made art packs.

  • Fair monetisation

    Our games are free. Watching a rewarded ad is your choice, there are no banner ads, and every shop pack says plainly what it gives.

  • Respect for privacy

    We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play. We say plainly what we process and why in our privacy policy.

The studio

Cem Tezgelen founded Symbol Interactive in Aydın in 2026. We design, write, draw and publish our games ourselves; we are not a big team, and we touch every detail of every game.
