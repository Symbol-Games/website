In short

  • We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play; the game creates an anonymous account for you.
  • Your progress is kept on your phone and in a cloud save in Europe (Belgium). You can delete your account in the game at any time.
  • In Türkiye, the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics and personalised ads run only if you allow them.
  • If you are under 16 (under 18 in Türkiye), you are not asked for consent; analytics and personalised ads stay off.
  • Apple and Google handle purchases; we never see your card details. We don't sell your information for money.
  • Test versions 0.56.0 and earlier collect no personal data; from 0.62.0, test versions follow this policy too.

What this policy covers

This policy covers Symbol Interactive's mobile game Star Jars Sort Deluxe (Yıldız Kavanozu in Turkish) on iOS and Android from version 0.62.0 (test versions included): what information it processes and why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what your rights are. It is our privacy notice under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the information the GDPR requires for players in Europe. When we release a new game, we add its details to this page.

Test versions. Test versions 0.56.0 and earlier have no analytics, ads, account or cloud save and collect no personal data for us; progress stays on the phone. From 0.62.0, test versions include the analytics (with your consent), crash reports and ads described in this policy; cloud save and account linking arrive with 0.63.0. If you join a test, we use your email address to invite you. Apple and Google may pass on feedback you send through TestFlight or Google Play and crash reports from test versions; we use them only to fix bugs.

This website. symbolinteractive.com.tr uses no cookies, does not track visitors and loads no fonts or scripts from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process your IP address in its own logs when serving the pages (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Data controller and contact

The data controller is Cem Tezgelen, an individual developer making games as Symbol Interactive, Aydın, Türkiye.

For any question or request: symbolinteractive@gmail.com. If you want to apply in writing by post, ask us for our postal address by email.

What information we process, and why

Information is collected automatically while the game runs, through the game and the service providers' libraries (SDKs) inside it; and by email if you write to us. Which information is processed where and on what basis is in legal bases and consent.

Age question

When the game first opens it asks for your year of birth. Only your age group is kept on your phone (under 13, under 16 — under 18 in Türkiye — or older); neither your year of birth nor your age group is sent to us or anyone else. Your age group decides which permissions are asked and which features stay off (children and teenagers).

Account

When the game first opens, Google Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account for you. All it has is a random ID, shown in Settings → Cloud save as your player ID. If you're signed in to Game Center on iPhone or to Google Play Games on Android, the game links the account to it by itself, without opening any window. If you like, you can also link it to Sign in with Apple in Settings → Cloud save (on iPhone and Android; that's how your progress moves between the two). Firebase then receives the account or player ID that Apple, Game Center or Google gives for this game, and the basic profile details the provider sends with the sign-in (such as your Google Play Games gamer name). We don't ask for your name or email when you link. We use these only to recognise you and to bring your progress to another phone. Firebase Authentication keeps a log of IP addresses for a few weeks to protect accounts from abuse.

Cloud save

So that you don't lose your progress and can take it to a new phone, your save is stored with your account in Google Cloud Firestore, in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, in the European Union). It holds your game progress (levels, stars, stardust and your items), a progress summary (level, stars and stardust), your device type (iPhone, iPad or Android), the game's version and when it was saved. Your settings, the permissions you gave and the store's records stay on your phone and never go to the cloud. Only your own account can read or write it. A copy of your progress also stays on your phone.

Purchases

The App Store or Google Play takes the payment; we never see your card details. From the store we receive only the purchase receipt (which product, the transaction ID, the time), which we check on your phone to give you the item and to restore it when needed. If analytics is on, the amount and currency of a purchase also go to Firebase Analytics for our revenue reports.

Ads

The game shows ads from ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob through Unity LevelPlay: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a reward, and interstitial ads, which appear between levels after some wins. There are no banner ads. To choose, show and count ads and to prevent fraud, these services process your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, device information (such as model, operating system and language), how you interact with ads and, if you allow it, your advertising ID (the advertising ID on Android; the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow app tracking). For personalised ads, ad partners may combine this with information from other apps and websites to guess your interests; without personalisation, ads are not chosen by your interests. We never give your game account or player ID to ad partners. The Remove Ads purchase turns interstitial ads off; rewarded ads stay, for whenever you want one. If analytics is on, we measure ad revenue (ad network, ad format, amount) in Firebase Analytics.

Analytics

To improve the game and balance its difficulty we use Google Firebase Analytics. Analytics processes how you interact with the game (screens opened, levels started, won and lost, session length, purchases, ads watched), device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language), an ID unique to the game's copy on this phone, and the approximate location derived from your IP address (country and city level). Analytics does not collect the advertising ID, and this information is not linked to your game account, name or email. In Türkiye, the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, Analytics runs only if you allow it; if you don't, it collects nothing.

Crash reports

To find and fix bugs we use Firebase Crashlytics. When the game crashes or logs an error, Crashlytics receives the error log, diagnostic information (device model, operating system and app version, memory state, time) and a random installation ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, crash reports are sent only if you allow it.

Remote settings

With Firebase Remote Config we change some of the game's settings (how often interstitial ads appear, for example) without releasing an update. The settings are the same for everyone, not chosen per person. For this, Firebase uses an ID unique to this installation of the game (the Firebase installation ID).

First-steps counter. To understand how the game's first minutes go, we count a few steps once per installation (the game opening, the tour ending, the first levels being won). Only the step's name, the operating system and the game version are sent, with no ID, advertising ID or account details. All we keep is a total per day and step, with no record of who anyone is. For the request to arrive, your IP address reaches Google's server and may stay in its server logs for up to 30 days; we are working on switching this off as well. The legal basis is legitimate interest. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the counter only runs after you allow analytics, and it does not run if you told us you are under 13.

Sky window. If you turn on the camera in the sky window, the picture is only shown behind the stars on your phone's screen: we don't save it or process it, and it doesn't leave your phone. The camera permission is asked only when you tap the camera button; without it the window works with no camera. We don't ask for your location: we guess your city from your phone's time zone or you pick one from a list, and your choice stays on this phone, out of the cloud save, and never reaches us. Your phone's direction is used on the phone at that moment to turn the sky on the screen; it isn't kept or sent. Sky event reminders (meteor showers, eclipses) are scheduled on your phone and nothing reaches us; they aren't scheduled if Settings → Notifications is off or you told us you're under 13.

Notifications. If you allowed notifications on your phone and Settings → Notifications is on, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send news and events from the game. For this, Firebase creates a notification token unique to this installation of the game. We do not keep this token in a list of players: notifications go to topics, for everyone and by language. Turning notifications off in Settings takes you off the topics and deletes the token. We do not send notifications to players we know are under 13.

Clock check. To check the time for daily rewards, the game now and then sends a short request to this site's server or, if it can't reach it, to Google's Firebase server, and reads only the time in the answer. The request carries no player ID or account details. As with any internet connection, the server sees the IP address during the request; we don't match it to a player.

Friends and the ranking

Nothing in this section is sent unless you open Friends or the daily puzzle's ranking. The first time you do, we create a friends profile tied to your account. It holds your star code, the nickname you picked from a preset list, your avatar, your level, your star count, your best move count per level, the last day you played, your friends list, the lives you sent and received, the players you blocked, and your moves and tries in the daily puzzle. It is stored in the same place as your cloud save (europe-west1).

Your friends see your nickname, avatar, level, stars, best moves per level, the last day you played and your daily puzzle result. Adding a friend takes your star code, and nobody becomes a friend until the other player accepts. If you turn off Visible to others, friends see only your nickname and avatar, and you don't appear on the world ranking. On the world ranking, players see each other's nickname, avatar, moves and tries. There are no typed names, no chat and no messages. You can remove or block a friend at any time, and turn Friends off in Settings.

These features are off for players who say they are under 13, and none of this information is created for them.

Your consent choices

The permissions you give or refuse are stored on your phone, so that you aren't asked at every launch and so that they can be passed on to ad partners.

Support emails

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer you and carry out your request.

Our legitimate interest is keeping the game working, safe, balanced and free to play. You can object to this processing.

In other countries analytics and crash reports rely on our legitimate interests; personalised ads follow your phone's settings (app tracking on iOS, the advertising ID on Android) and the opt-out rights your country's law gives you.

What is required and what is not. The account ID and the cloud save are part of the service; without them the cloud save doesn't work. Analytics, crash reports where we ask, and personalised ads are optional; refusing them doesn't stop you playing.

Automated decisions. We make no decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects on you. If you allow personalised ads, ad partners build a profile to guess your interests.

Where and how we ask for consent

  • Türkiye: on first launch the game asks two separate questions on its own consent card: analytics, and personalised ads. Both start off.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent message asks about ad partners and ad personalisation; the game's consent card asks about analytics and crash reports.
  • US states with privacy laws: Google's state message lets you opt out of your information being used for personalised ads (a "sale" or "sharing" in those laws' words).
  • iOS: the advertising ID (IDFA) is used only if you allow app tracking when Apple's prompt asks. If you refused personalised ads, or you are under age, the prompt is never shown.
  • Under 16 (under 18 in Türkiye): no consent is asked; analytics and personalised ads stay off.

You can change your choice at any time: wherever the game asks for consent, a Privacy options row appears in its Settings. Everywhere, you can also use your phone's settings: on iOS, turn off tracking for the game in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in the ads section of your phone's settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

Who receives it

We don't sell your information to anyone for money. For the purposes above, information goes to these companies:

  • Google (Google LLC and its affiliates). For Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Analytics, Google is our processor, acting on our behalf and instructions. For AdMob and Google Play, Google is a separate controller that also uses information for its own purposes. Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and security in Firebase, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Unity (Unity Technologies and its affiliates; ironSource is a Unity company): LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads. Unity is a separate controller of ad data. Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Apple (Apple Inc. and its affiliates): the App Store, purchases and Sign in with Apple; Apple is a separate controller. Apple Privacy Policy.

Other players. If you use Friends or the ranking, your friends and the players on the ranking see the information listed under "Friends and the ranking" above. Your real name, email and player ID do not go to them.

If a law or a decision of a competent authority requires it, we also share information with the competent authorities.

Transfers abroad

Your information is processed outside Türkiye: your cloud save in the European Union (Belgium), your account record (Firebase Authentication) in the United States, and analytics, crash reports, remote settings and ad data on Google's and Unity's servers in several countries, mainly the United States. These transfers are made for the purposes above and only to the companies above. Transfers from Türkiye are subject to Article 9 of the KVKK.

For players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, transfers to the United States rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where the recipient is certified (such as Google), and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (such as Unity). You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Account and cloud save: until you delete them. If the game isn't opened with an account for 24 months, we delete that account and its cloud save.
  • Friends profile: deleted with your account. Lives waiting for you are deleted after 7 days.
  • Daily puzzle ranking: 7 days.
  • Account IP logs: a few weeks.
  • Analytics: event data is deleted after 14 months. Aggregate figures made from it that identify no one (daily player counts, for example) may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Firebase installation ID: deleted by Firebase after 270 days without use.
  • Deleted data: Google clears deleted account data from its live and backup systems within 180 days.
  • Ad partners: for the periods in their own policies (linked above).
  • Purchases: the items you bought are kept as part of your progress, as above. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
  • Support emails: deleted within a year of the matter being closed.
  • Your age group and consent choices: on your phone, until you delete the game.

When a period ends, the information is deleted or made anonymous so that it identifies no one.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you can: learn whether your personal data is processed; if it is, ask for information about it; learn why it is processed and whether it is used for that purpose; know who receives it in Türkiye or abroad; have it corrected if it is incomplete or wrong; have it deleted or destroyed where the law's conditions are met; have corrections and deletions passed on to whoever received it; object to a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation if unlawful processing has harmed you.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the GDPR and similar laws also give you the right to access your information, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw your consent at any time. If the law where you live gives you other rights (for example the LGPD in Brazil), you can use them the same way.

How to apply

  • In the game: delete your account and cloud save yourself in Settings → Cloud save → Delete account.
  • By email: write to symbolinteractive@gmail.com with the player ID shown in the game's Settings; we find your information by that ID.
  • In writing: ask us for our postal address by email.

We answer within 30 days, free of charge, in writing (by email). If we need to be sure who you are, we may ask for more information.

Complaints. If your application is refused, you find our answer inadequate or we don't answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board in Türkiye within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your application. If you are in Europe, you can complain to the data protection authority of the country you live in.

Players in the United States

We don't sell your information for money. But some US state laws (such as California's CCPA) count ad partners receiving identifiers for personalised advertising as a "sale" or "sharing". In that legal sense, we may "sell" or "share" these categories of information with Unity and Google for personalised ads: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), in-app activity (how you interact with ads) and approximate location. You can opt out in the game's Settings → Privacy options row, or in your phone's ad settings.

The categories of information we collect, their sources (you and your phone), purposes and retention periods are in the sections above. We collect no sensitive personal information. We do not knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16.

Your state's law may give you the right to know what information we collect, to have it corrected or deleted, to opt out of sale and sharing, and to appeal a refused request. Just email us. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and cloud save in the game: Settings → Cloud save → Delete account. If you can't open the game, you can email us. What is deleted, what remains and how long it takes is on the account deletion page.

Children and teenagers

Our games are for ages 13 and up; they are not directed at children under 13. The game asks for your year of birth the first time it needs it (before asking for a permission) and acts on it; "I'd rather not say" counts as under age:

  • Under 16 (under 18 in Türkiye): no consent is asked; analytics and personalised ads stay off and the iOS tracking prompt is not shown. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland crash reports are not sent either. Ads are not chosen by your interests.
  • Under 13: in addition, ad partners are told to treat you as a child, and no account is linked (Game Center, Apple or Google).

If you think a child under 13 has given us information, write to symbolinteractive@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels between your phone and the servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Only your own account can read or write your cloud save. We never see your card details. If a data breach happens, we notify the authorities and, where needed, you, as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page with its new date. If a new purpose needs your consent, we ask for it again.
